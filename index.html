Monitor your Mac from the menubar

iStat Menus places essential system monitoring directly in your menubar. Eight precise menu extras track CPU, memory, disks, network, temperatures, fans, Bluetooth, date & time, and weather—keeping you informed without distraction.

CPU & Memory

Track CPU usage across seven display modes with full multi-core support. Monitor memory in four modes, including page ins/outs and swap—optimized for clarity at a glance.

Disks & Network

Watch disk usage and activity in six modes; hide irrelevant drives. Network monitoring shows current and total bandwidth, peaks, IP addresses—filter connections effortlessly.

Sensors & Fans

Monitor Mac temperatures and fan speeds in two modes each; conceal unneeded sensors. Bluetooth controls status and battery for wireless devices.

Time & Weather

World clocks for multiple locations. Weather delivers current temperature, hourly forecasts, weekly overviews—elegantly integrated.
